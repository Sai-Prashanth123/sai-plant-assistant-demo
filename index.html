How it's built

Safe, official and careful

  • Official WhatsApp Cloud API, so there's no ban risk
  • Tips sent as Meta-approved templates, which is required after WhatsApp's 24-hour window
  • Answers only from your care guides
  • Photo checks show a confidence level; unsure cases go to a person
  • PAUSE / STOP honoured instantly
